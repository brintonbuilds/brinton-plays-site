The story behind the song

It started with muddy running shoes and a talk before bed.

“Quarter Past Eight” is about my son and the end of the day. Shoes off, lights down, and the questions he saves for when the house goes quiet. Most of them are small. Some of them aren’t.

It’s a promise, plain and simple. However old you get, whatever life throws at you, I’ll make the time to listen.

The man behind the music

I’m Andrew Brinton. Husband, father and a late starter at songwriting.

I sang around the house for years before I ever thought about recording. Then a trip to Newfoundland in 2026 shook something loose. The people, the coastline, the family stories, the slower pace. I came home with songs I had to write.

I write about family, getting older, what we carry and what we can’t afford to forget.

Nothing fancy. Honest stories and an acoustic guitar.
